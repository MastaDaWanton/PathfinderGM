Icons made by Lorc (http://lorcblog.blogspot.com), Delapouite (http://delapouite.com),
Skoll, Sbed (http://opengameart.org/content/95-game-icons), Willdabeast, Carl Olsen,
Caro Asercion and Lucas, from game-icons.net.

Changes made: the black background square of each icon was removed and the glyph's fill
set to "currentColor", so the app can tint it to the page's palette. The shapes are
unchanged. Each file is named <author>-<icon>.svg.

Fetched 2026-09-29 from the official repository, https://github.com/game-icons/icons,
through its jsDelivr mirror (https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/game-icons/icons@master/).
