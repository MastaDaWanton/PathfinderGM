How to read the table. Each group is one piece of machinery. **Finishes** counts the feats whose every missing clause is in that group, so building that group alone makes them work; **touches** counts every feat with at least one line in it. Groups run in descending order of feats finished. Size: S is a day or less, M a few days, L a whole subsystem. *Buildable by the book* means the Core Rulebook or the feat's own book defines the rule fully; *needs a design call* means a subsystem or a choice that is the owner's to make.

### 1. Moves spliced around an action (move-shoot-move, step between cleaves, move after a coup, swift moves)

Buildable by the book; size L. Finishes 9, touches 9. One turn holds one move before or after its blow today; the book splits a move around a single action.

- `cartwheel-dodge` (b4-moves.json): 'When you successfully use improved evasion to avoid taking damage, you can move up to half your speed as an immediate action': the save that evades is rolled with no square to move to; no move is offered after it
- `cleave-through` (batch2-attack-a.json): a free 5-foot step between Cleave's attacks that brings a new foe into reach: Cleave's extra blows are chosen from those threatened when it is declared
- `combat-patrol` (b4-moves.json): 'You may move as part of these attacks, provided your total movement before your next turn does not exceed your speed': an attack of opportunity is swung from where its maker stands; no step is spliced in front of it
- `cut-your-losses` (batch2-attack-b.json): picking up an unattended object or an unconscious ally during a full-round withdraw without provoking: the withdraw picks nothing up on the way
- `disengaging-flourish` (batch2-attack-a.json): a standard-action Bluff against every foe threatening you, then a move at speed that provokes none of those fooled
- `shot-on-the-run` (b4-combat.json): 'As a full-round action, you can move up to your speed and make a single ranged attack at any point during your movement': a turn moves once, before or after its blow; no walk is split around a shot (Spring Attack's split walk is melee's, closing on its foe)

- `tiger-pounce` (batch2-styles.json): Power Attack's penalty taken on AC instead of the attack roll, and the swift move of half your speed toward a foe you hit: no option moves Power Attack's penalty, and a swift move is not priced
- `wand-dancer` (b4-moves.json): 'you can move both before and after triggering the item, as long as the total distance moved is not greater than your speed ... does not provoke attacks of opportunity from that creature only': no split move is kept around a use
