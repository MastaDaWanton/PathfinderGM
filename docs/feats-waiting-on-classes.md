## Class features no playable class has (feat loop batch 2)

The feat's prerequisites read as takeable, but the clause it changes belongs to a class
feature no playable class grants.

- Rebounding Leap: leaping lance (an archetype's; the content has no archetypes)
- Thoughtful Discernment: discern lies (inquisitor)
- Adder Strike: poison use (alchemist, assassin)
- Pinpoint Poisoner: poison use (alchemist, assassin)
- Revelation Strike: an oracle's revelation
- Kobold Flood: Kobold Groundling, a kobold feat

- Renown: Fame and Prestige Points (Pathfinder Society play)

- Equipment Trick: equipment tricks (Adventurer's Armory, Magical Marketplace)

- Conviction: a vampire's aversion to a holy symbol — the bestiary's vampire weaknesses are

- Improved Stalwart: Stalwart, which batch 1 left without a document (trading a defensive
  stance's dodge bonus for DR is not built)
- Dimensional Savant: Dimensional Dervish's teleporting full attack, still a `not_yet` in
  batch2-attack-a.json

## Feat loop batch 5, lane b5-maneuver

Confirmed again (listed above, still waiting on the same thing): Rebounding Leap, Thoughtful
Discernment, Adder Strike, Pinpoint Poisoner, Revelation Strike, Kobold Flood, Murderer's
Circle, Focused Overseer, Dramatic Display, Dragoncrafting, Tandemevasion.

A prerequisite nobody can take:

- Greater Serpent Lash: Serpent Lash, which is not takeable (its prerequisite line does not
  parse, so the picker never offers it), and every clause of the greater feat changes a
  Serpent Lash manoeuvre
- Dimensional Maneuvers: Dimensional Dervish's teleporting full attack, still a `not_yet`
  in batch2-attack-a.json (Dimensional Savant waits on the same)
- Net Trickery: net attacks (the net is unwieldable; Net Adept waits on the same)

Systems not in the game:

- Brutal Grappler: two creatures holding one in a grapple — a grapple has one holder, and
  a second grapple at a held creature is the holder's maintain check
- Disorienting Maneuver: tumbling through an opponent's space (CRB Acrobatics, DC = CMD
  + 5); the tumble door moves past threatened squares only
- Tactical Reposition: a push or reposition into a trap or hazard square never sets it off
  (`_push_line` moves the body through `_put`, which asks no ward)
- Undermine: difficult terrain for one creature alone — the board's difficult squares are
  everybody's

## Feat loop batch 5, lane b5-spells

Class, race or class feature no player can take:

- Prophetic Visionary: the mystery class feature (oracle) — confirmed again
- Kobold Groundling: race kobold (its Kobold Style prerequisite)
- Familiar Spell: a familiar in play — the wizard's arcane bond names a familiar as a choice
  word, but no familiar is a creature that could hold and cast a spell

Systems not in the game:

- Ascendant Spell: mythic spells (confirmed again)
- Stable Spell: primal magic areas and their events (Mythic Adventures' wild zones)
- Blighted Critical, Greater Blighted Critical: spellblights (Ultimate Magic pp. 95-96)
- Spell Mastery: a spellbook in hand — preparing never asks a wizard for the book, so the
  feat's "without referring to a spellbook" changes nothing until a book can be lost
- Inspirational Commander: armies and Morale checks (Ultimate Campaign's mass combat)
- Siege Commander: siege engines and their assembly crews (as Siege Gunner)
- Ostentatious Display: valuable nonmagical items worn in a magic item slot, and wealth by
  level (Table 12-4) to price them against
- Childlike: disguise's age and race modifiers (rules/disguise.py says they are not built)
  and taking 10 on a Bluff check
- Altitude Affinity: altitude — no place says how high it lies, so neither acclimation nor
  "5,000 feet or higher" can be asked
- Deadly Dealer: a deck of cards as thrown weapons (no card is a weapon in the gear)
