Yarrow. Leaves prepared in a poultice and bound on a wound heal 1d4 hit points. Powdered and brewed, they break a fever, a +1 alchemical bonus on Fortitude saves against fever, and help with reading what is to come, +1 on Sense Motive checks, each for an hour.

**Effects.** Heals 1d4 hit points (wound); +1 Fortitude against fever for 1 hour (ingest); +1 Sense Motive for 1 hour (ingest)
