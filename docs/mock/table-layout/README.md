# Table layout mock

A clickable layout study for the play table, to set beside the real one. It is **not part
of the app**: no Django view, no URL, nothing under `play/`, and `docs/` is not bundled
into the executable (see `pathfindergm.spec`). Nothing on it reaches the engine or a model.

## Opening it

From the repository root:

```
python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

then open <http://127.0.0.1:8765/docs/mock/table-layout/>. It has to be served. Opened
as a file, the browser refuses the icon fetches, and the gear and spells grids come up
empty. The page links the real fonts, textures and icons under `play/static/` by relative
path, so it must be served from the root, not from this folder.

The **Mock** menu at the top right switches the character (Kesst Vayr, the recording's
rogue; Ysolde Marrach, the fixture wizard, to see Spells), marks the character wanted in
Zhilvarnia (to see shut ways and their reasons), and starts again at the gate.

## What is real

- **Exits**: `play/exits.py exits()` run on the Pangrella fixture for all 43 places in
  Zhilvarnia at 08:00, plain and wanted. Walking the row moves you through the real place
  graph, and every place shows its own ways on. The road to Xylorvotha offers the real
  five-day journey with its "Set out" confirmation.
- **Story, suggestions, people, faces**: `tests/replay/2026-09-25-town-tags-retag-gemma4-12b.jsonl.gz`
  (a recorded session against the fixture world, already in the repository). The two
  beats are verbatim, including the model's own dashes. The player's private corpora are
  not used.
- **Numbers**: `pc.summary()` for `fixtures/pc-kesst.json` and `fixtures/pc-caster.json`.
  The wizard's prepared list is what `casting.ensure_prepared` gives her.
- **Gear**: the fixture's weapons, armour and free outfit. The rest (thieves' tools, pack,
  rope and so on) is an example outfitter basket, and each tile says so when read.
- **Journal**: the recording's matter names and the lines the smith and the guard spoke.

`data.js` is generated by `build_data.py` (run it from the repository root). Every reply
to a click is a canned line in the out-of-character style, beginning "Mock.", never prose
made up to look like the narrator's.

## The design decisions

- **Redesign, preserve.** The app's `:root` tokens copied verbatim, its textures
  (`grimoire-leather`, `card-leather`, `leather-tile`), its gilt gradient and clasps,
  Cinzel and the Palatino stack, and its radius scale (2px controls, 3px surfaces, 12px
  suggestion seals, round medallions). No new accent colour. One lighter step of `--dim`
  inside the leather sides, because the app's own value measured 3.0:1 there.
- **The story is the centrepiece.** A gilt-framed book in the middle, with the clasps at
  its corners and a drop cap on each GM passage. The measure is 58ch at 18px, which
  measured 67 characters a line on the recorded beat (66ch had measured 76). The book
  keeps at least 45% of the height. Past that, the desk's choices scroll and the pen stays
  put: at 1024x768 the story had been down to three lines.
- **One sheet, split round the story.** Who the character is on the left (portrait, name,
  gear) and their numbers on the right (life, medallions, defence, saves, experience).
  Nothing appears on both sides. "Hide the sheet" hides both, and the story centres at the
  same measure. Below 1180px the two halves share one column. The Sheet tab keeps both
  sides and fills the middle with the rest (skills, feats, background, notes).
- **Moves come from the exits, and everything else is a chip.** The "From here" row
  (Next door, Outside, Roads) holds every move, with its time in words. When a whole group
  shares one time, it is said once under the group's name. A shut way stays in the row,
  dashed, and its reason appears in a line held open beneath it. The suggestion chips hold
  everything that is not a move, and a chip fills the pen as it does in the app. Each
  choice appears once. The app already drops bare-move suggestions that duplicate an exit
  (`11-exits.js`).
- **Labelled modes.** Table, Sheet, Spells, Trade, Journal: each is a word, with arrow-key
  tab behaviour. The desk keeps Say, Continue, Craft action, Trade and Spells. Continue
  stays, by the owner's ruling (the scene moves a beat). The footer Spells only appears
  for a caster, as in the app. It attaches a spell to the turn, which is a different job
  from the Spells tab's grimoire.
- **Portrait and icon inventory.** An upload slot with a local preview (nothing is
  stored). Gear is an icon grid of the repo's own game-icons.net SVGs, tinted by
  `currentColor` and fetched inline, as the app does because of the Windows mime trap.
  Icons are picked by the app's own tables, and the credits sit under the grid.
- **Ability medallions.** A gilt ring, the score struck in Cinzel, the modifier on a
  plaque at the foot, with the full name for screen readers.
- **Motion.** None. There are no transitions or animations anywhere. Hover changes colour
  only, and the toggle keeps its width so the tabs never move. The popovers (Craft action,
  Spells) open over the story's foot rather than into the desk, and the lines for shut
  ways and journeys are reserved in advance, so nothing shifts under the pointer.
- **Phone (375px).** The tabs stay on top. The book's head scrolls away with the story
  (the fixed head left 190px of reading). Each exit group and the chips become one
  sideways-scrolling row, so the desk stays short. A "Life 9 of 9, AC 15" button in the
  book's head opens the Sheet, which is the drawer idea as a mode: the whole sheet in one
  column, with the portrait beside the name.

## Deliberately not taken from the owner's mockup

- **The slate palette.** The app's leather, gilt and candle-gold stay.
- **The gargoyle and skull ornament.** The only ornament is what the app already ships:
  the gilt edge and the clasps.
- **Map-anchored choices.** The owner: "forget being anchored to a map and give the
  choices based on entrances". There is no map on this page, and moves come from the
  engine's graph.
- **Duplicated choices.** A move is an exit button and never also a chip.
- **Two stat sidebars.** The mockup repeated the stats on both sides. Here the one sheet
  is split, not doubled.
- **The unlabelled icon toolbar and the ~330px story column.**

## Open questions for the owner

- The footer's **Trade** does the same thing as the Trade tab. It is kept because it was
  on the list, but two doors to one room may be one too many.
- The **Map** and **Talk** trays are left out of this layout. Journal holds what was said.
  Whether the conversation tray becomes Journal, or stays a tray, is open.
