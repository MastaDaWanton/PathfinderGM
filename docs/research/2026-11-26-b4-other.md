Feat text: `content/feats/feats.json` (the PRD's text), checked against aonprd.com

## Rules read for the engine defects

- **Mindless.** Ooze type (aonprd.com/MonsterTypes.aspx?ItemName=Ooze): "Mindless: No
  Intelligence score, and immunity to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions,
  phantasms, patterns, and morale effects)", and "An ooze with an Intelligence score loses
  this trait." Vermin carry the same Mindless line. So the rule is the missing Int score,
  not the type: `states.immune_to` asks `morale.intelligence` and answers "mindless".
  Measured in the shipped bestiary: 19 oozes and 2 vermin print an Int score, and the ones
  the book makes immune print it themselves (the shoggoth's "charm effects", the slithering
  tracker's "mind-affecting effects"). A type bundle would have made all of them immune.
- **Fear is mind-affecting.** Every fear spell is "[fear, mind-affecting]"; undead are immune
  to "all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, and
  phantasms)" (Bestiary p.309). The undead and construct bundles had no fear word.
- **"mind-affecting effects".** 160 shipped blocks print the plural; no immunity row was
  keyed by it, so each was immune only to that literal phrase.
- **The orc's subtype.** Bestiary 1 p.222 as printed (read off the PDF with pypdf): "Orc
  warrior 1 / CE Medium humanoid" — no "(orc)". The class line names the people; the
  extractor now takes that word when another humanoid block prints it as a subtype.

## Senses

- Scent (aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Scent): "detect opponents within 30 feet by sense of
  smell ... When the creature is within 5 feet of the source, it pinpoints the source's
  location." Wind direction (60/15 ft) is not modelled: no wind direction exists.
- Tremorsense (aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Tremorsense): "can automatically pinpoint the
  location of anything that is in contact with the ground."

## Prior art

- Rerolls and roll-twice: Foundry PF1 leaves them to the player's hand (a chat button);
  Owlcat's CRPGs hook the d20 event (`RuleRollD20`). The existing `rules/luck.py` follows
  the Owlcat shape for everybody but the player, who is asked on the dice popup; the new
  kinds (`confirm_against`, `twice`, `while`) are the same shape.
- Meditation Master: no CRPG or VTT models it beyond a manual buff toggle (Foundry PF1's
  community buff compendium). Folded into the night, as the owner's Q40 did for a wizard's
  study hour.
