# Lane A research: spell buffs as timed ActiveEffects (2026-11-22)

What the buff executor (`Engine._cast_buff`) follows, where it came from, and what could
not be sourced.

## Rules text (primary)

- **Willing targets forgo the save.** "A creature can voluntarily forgo a saving throw and
  willingly accept a spell's result" (CRB, Magic, Saving Throws; d20pfsrd.com/magic, read
  2026-11-22). Used by `Engine._willing`: enlarge person, reduce person and their mass forms
  on the caster or an ally land with no roll.
- **Harmless.** "The spell is usually beneficial, not harmful, but a targeted creature can
  attempt a saving throw if it desires" (same page). Harmless spells already roll no save.
- **Stacking.** Spells "usually do not stack with themselves"; "two bonuses of the same type
  don't stack even if they come from different spells" (same page, Combining Magical
  Effects). Implemented by the existing funnel: same source refreshes (`Actor.add_buff`),
  same named type takes the best (`dice.stack`).
- **Aid** (aonprd.com SpellDisplay "Aid", read 2026-11-22): +1 morale on attack rolls and on
  saves against fear, 1d8 + caster level temporary hit points (caster-level part maximum
  +10), 1 minute/level.
- **Divine Favor** (aonprd.com SpellDisplay "Divine Favor", read 2026-11-22): luck bonus on
  attack and weapon damage rolls, +1 per three caster levels, minimum +1, maximum +3, not on
  spell damage, 1 minute.
- Bless (allies), bane (enemies), prayer (allies +1 luck, foes -1), heroism, good hope,
  crushing despair, rage, haste, slow, expeditious retreat, longstrider, false life,
  divine power: read from the spell text the corpus carries (`content/spells/spells.json`,
  the Codex's CRB text). Not re-fetched one by one.

## Prior art (from the audit's §4, reused)

- Foundry PF1 abandoned a buff as a record linked to a second record (0.77 → 11.0): a buff
  is one effect holding its typed modifiers. Here: each term is one `ActiveEffect` through
  `Actor.add_buff` / `gain_temp_hp` / `grant_defence`, removed by the one ticker.
- Dice never enter a stored total as dice (Foundry 10.0 / 0.80.21): aid's 1d8 + CL is
  rolled once at cast by the `temp_hp` op and the total kept.
- Duration from one source (Foundry 10.x): `spells.roll_duration` via `_cast_context`.
- No scripts in data (Foundry, PCGen, Fantasy Grounds): the per-caster half of aid's and
  false life's temporary hit points is a validated `signed_formula` field (`bonus`) on
  `temp_hp`, not code.

## Decisions taken by the book (owner calls)

- **A spec with a `note` is told, not applied.** A note is the document qualifying the
  number in words ("against fear effects", "+1 per three caster levels"); reading it with a
  regex is the shape the owner refused (models-read-code-validates). The fix is in the data.
- **In an area, a bonus lands on the caster's side and a penalty on the other**
  (`Engine._against`). Right for bless, bane and prayer as printed. Crushing despair's cone
  catches allies too by RAW, but it is a save gate, so its targets are decided by the cone
  and each rolls — the side rule only governs ungated area buffs.
- **No map, an area buff centred on the caster reaches everybody here** (on the right side),
  as `_op_use_item`'s bursts read an unmeasured area. `areas.caught` catches only a named
  aim without a map, so bless said at nobody blessed nobody.
- **The willing exception is narrow.** Only a gate whose failure branch is all buffs or
  narrative, with at least one positive amount (enlarge person's +2 Str beside its -2 Dex),
  and only for a creature on the caster's side. Hold person on a friend still asks the save;
  crushing despair's penalties alone are not "willingly accepted".

## Could not source / not built

- How Foundry PF1 implements "until discharged" (stoneskin's 10/level cap, protection from
  arrows' cap): not confirmed. Not built here; those specs stay told.
- A single-roll bonus (true strike, guidance: `uses: once`) needs something that spends it
  on the next roll. Nothing does; told.
