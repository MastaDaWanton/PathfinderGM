# Lane E research: skill uses with DC tables (2026-11-22)

Read before building, per the standing instruction. What was used, where, and what could
not be sourced.

## Rules text (primary, d20pfsrd.com, fetched 2026-11-22)

- **Feint.** Bluff page: DC "10 + your opponent's base attack bonus + your opponent's Wisdom
  modifier", or "10 + your opponent's Sense Motive bonus, if higher" when the opponent is
  trained; "Feinting in combat is a standard action"; retry yes. Combat page, Feint:
  non-humanoid -4; "a creature of animal Intelligence (1 or 2)" -8; "Against a creature
  lacking an Intelligence score, it's impossible"; "the next melee attack you make against
  the target does not allow him to use his Dexterity bonus to AC (if any). This attack must
  be made on or before your next turn."
- **Improved Feint**: "You can make a Bluff check to feint in combat as a move action."
  **Greater Feint**: the opponent "loses that bonus until the beginning of your next turn,
  in addition to losing his Dexterity bonus against your next attack."
- **Acrobatics**: DC = the opponent's CMD to move through a threatened area (5 + CMD through
  an enemy's space); "+2 for each additional opponent avoided in one round"; half speed, or
  full speed at +10; no tumbling in medium or heavy armour or under a medium or heavy load
  that slows you; Action: none, part of the move.
- **Knowledge**: "the DC of such a check equals 10 + the monster's CR. For common monsters,
  such as goblins, the DC of this check equals 5 + the monster's CR"; one useful fact, one
  more per 5 over; untrained, no DC above 10; no retry. Type table: Arcana constructs,
  dragons, magical beasts; Dungeoneering aberrations, oozes; Local humanoids; Nature animals,
  fey, monstrous humanoids, plants, vermin; Planes outsiders; Religion undead.
  (play/gm_answers.py had vermin and monstrous humanoids under Dungeoneering; the row
  follows the CRB.)
- **Heal**: first aid a standard action, retry allowed; long-term care DC 15 over 8 hours,
  "as many as six patients at a time", "You cannot give long-term care to yourself"; treat
  deadly wounds needs two uses of a healer's kit. Bleed (the condition): "stopped by a DC 15
  Heal check or through the application of any spell that cures hit point damage".
- **Dazzling Display**: Weapon Focus prerequisite; "While wielding the weapon in which you
  have Weapon Focus ... as a full-round action. Make an Intimidate check to demoralize all
  foes within 30 feet who can see your display."

## Prior art

- **Owlcat (Kingmaker)** kept bonuses able to aim at one use of a merged skill (audit §4,
  via RPG Codex). That is why a row's id, `skill.<skill>.<use>`, is what a feat document's
  tag names here (`skill.bluff.feint.move-action`).
- **Foundry PF1 / PCGen** treat feint as a context note on the sheet; neither applies its
  consequence (audit §4 found no executor). Foundry's per-attack conditionals failed in the
  "only the first attack" shape (0.81.1), so the feint's first-swing-only rule has its own
  test.
- **Could not confirm** how Owlcat's Wrath models feint internally (one search, community
  posts only). Not relied on.

## Choices made by the book where nobody has ruled (owner calls)

1. Non-humanoid -4 and animal-Int -8 do not stack: an animal takes -8 only.
2. Common monsters' DC 5 + CR is not used: nothing in the bestiary says which are common,
   so every creature is DC 10 + CR.
3. One tumble roll is set against every foe the move leaves, cheapest CMD first (+2 each
   after), the reading kindest to the tumbler.
4. A feint outside a fight is refused, not opened into one.
5. A made first aid on somebody both dying and bleeding does both jobs (one DC 15 check).
6. "Magical healing" is a heal stamped `spell:`, `ward:` or `ability:`; a draught or a herb
   (`item:`) is not, by `sheet.MAGIC_ORIGINS`' reading.
