Prior art, as the audit (§4) already summarised it and as used here:

Prior-art claims below are from the audit's §4 reading and from memory of the systems; the
Owlcat "resource restored when the rage ends" shape was searched for on 2026-11-22 and
**could not be confirmed** (no blueprint source found), and the PCGen/Foundry pool shapes
were not re-read today. They are stated as the shape followed, not as verified fact.

- **Pools raised by feats.** PCGen raises a pool with a `BONUS:VAR|<pool>|<n>` on the feat
  (the feat adds to a variable the class's ability reads); Foundry PF1 does the same with a
  Change on a resource formula. Here it is a typed modifier on the funnel target
  `pool.<id>`, read by `resources.define` — the same shape, through `dice.stack`, so an
  item or a timed effect can raise a pool too. Stacking copies of one feat are declared by
  the document (`stacks`), as the book declares them per feat.
- **Descriptors on saves.** Foundry PF1 carries a spell's school and descriptors on the
  roll and lets a context note or conditional modifier key on them; Owlcat's
  `SavingThrowBonusAgainstDescriptor` / `...AgainstSchool` components do the same. Here
  the save context's `against` became a set (`effectspec.spell_against`: "spell", school,
  subschool, descriptors), read off the spell document, never from the model.
- **Once per rage.** (Owlcat's model unconfirmed, see above.) Here a pool whose `refresh` is `stance.end.<key>`, refilled by `Scene.stance_ended`.

Corrected by the sweep: Might of the Gods is "an enhancement bonus to your Strength
score" (d20pfsrd Strength domain, fetched 2026-11-22), not to Strength checks as the
shipped power line said; the line and the new document say the score.

Could not source: whether "homing … ignores cover" (Heart-Seeker Spike, a homebrew class)
means an automatic hit — taken as a ranged attack that ignores cover, an owner call.
