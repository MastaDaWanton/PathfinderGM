# Feat loop b3-divine (2026-11-26): sources and the calls made

Lane list: `C:\Users\natha\pgm-work\feat-loop\b3-divine.tsv`, 34 feats. Each Benefit was
read from `content/feats/feats.json` (the OGL sheet, 2014-03-23) and checked against
aonprd.com FeatDisplay. Each document quotes the text it builds in its `source`.

The ones that needed looking up beyond the sheet:

- **Contingent Channeling** (UC p.93): its prerequisite "true healer class feature"
  belongs to the merciful healer cleric archetype (UC). The aonprd.com feat page names
  the feature without the archetype; a third-party archetype index confirmed which
  archetype. The app has no archetypes, so the feat waits.
- **Fabulist** (Champions of Balance): "Amateur Gunslinger feat or grit". Grit exists
  nowhere in the engine. The +1 Perform and the class skill are built; the grit clause is
  not.
- **Disruptive Recall** (magus) and **Righteous Healing** (inquisitor) need classes no
  player can take.

## Owner calls, taking the rules as written

1. Greater Channel Smite: the book lets the player split the dice pool across the turn's
   blows. The engine puts the whole pool on each blow until one lands. A miss keeps the
   dice, so a table that wants the dice to land splits them this way.
2. Turn Undead's re-save for the intelligent undead uses hold person's shape
   (`save_each_turn`). That shape spends the creature's turn on the try, which the feat
   does not say.
3. Bewildering Koan: the creature's check is Sense Motive against the Bluff, and "loses
   its next action" is dazed for a round.
4. Harmonic Sage: "inside an artificial structure" is any roofed place
   (`places.is_indoors`), caves included. The free check is always tried. On a success
   the engine takes Self-Harmonize for a performance with a save and Reverberation for
   one without.
5. Ensemble: a bard's "any one ally" is the nearest ally without the feat. Perform is one
   skill here, so "any category" means that one skill.
6. Discordant Voice: the bard's own blows are not an ally's.
7. Lingering Performance with Reverberation: the longer linger counts, and the two do
   not add.
8. Blood Vengeance on a raging barbarian lasts 4 rounds. The book says both "for the
   duration of your rage" and "In either case, this state lasts for 4 rounds", and the
   engine takes the second.
9. Elemental Channel's harm is positive energy.
10. Word of Healing heals another creature only, never the user and never the undead.
11. Radiant Charge's damage is typed `holy`. It is no energy, so no energy resistance or
    immunity applies, and it carries `ignores_dr`.
