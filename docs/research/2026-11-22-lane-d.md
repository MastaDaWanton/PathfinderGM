# Lane D research, 2026-11-22: attack-shape combat feats and extra attacks

What was looked up before building, what was taken, and what could not be sourced.

## Rules text (primary)

Every benefit quoted in `tests/test_attack_feats.py` and in the code is the Core Rulebook's,
read from the app's own `content/feats/feats.json` (the CRB/APG benefit text, checked
against aonprd.com/FeatDisplay.aspx) and, for the monk, from the CRB monk (aonprd.com
ClassDisplay Monk; the class-features file's `_about` records the fetch). The flurry's
numbers were checked against the CRB monk table's printed flurry column at 1st, 8th and
15th level, which the test pins.

- Combat Expertise, Deadly Aim, Piranha Strike: a declared penalty for a bonus, scaling every
  +4 base attack, "until your next turn".
- Vital Strike chain: the weapon's dice two, three, four times, "not multiplied on a critical
  hit, but ... added to the total", on the attack action only.
- Cleave / Great Cleave: on a hit, another blow at a foe adjacent to the previous and within
  reach; -2 AC until the next turn; Great Cleave chains and never strikes a foe twice.
- Rapid Shot: one more shot at the highest bonus on a full attack, all shots -2.
- Manyshot: the first shot of a full attack with a bow is two arrows; precision and critical
  damage once.
- Improved Critical: the threat range doubled, not stacking with keen (keen's own text).
- Critical Focus: +4 circumstance on confirmation rolls.
- Mobility: +4 dodge against attacks of opportunity for moving out of or within a threatened
  area.
- Two-Weapon Fighting (CRB Combat, Table 8-7): -6/-10, -4/-8 with a light off-hand weapon;
  the feat lessens them by 2 and 6; off hand at half Strength; Improved/Greater add the -5 and
  -10 off-hand attacks. Double Slice (APG): full Strength off hand.
- Mighty cleaving (CRB magic weapons): one more Cleave attack.

## Prior art

- **Owlcat (Kingmaker, Wrath of the Righteous)** model Power Attack, Deadly Aim, Combat
  Expertise and Piranha Strike as activatable toggles that stay on, and Vital Strike as an
  action ability. The bar's toggles here follow that shape (the toggle rides every blow
  staged after it). I could not find a primary Owlcat document for this; it is from play of
  the games and community wikis, so treat it as unconfirmed.
- **Foundry VTT PF1** keeps a fixed set of attacks for a full attack (gitlab issue 130 on
  Furyspark/foundryvtt-pathfinder1 says the count cannot be changed from the Attacks tab) and
  handles situational extras through its attack dialog and conditional modifiers. I could not
  confirm from primary source how it represents Rapid Shot or Manyshot; not used.
- **Forum consensus (not official rulings)**: Rapid Shot, haste and Manyshot all stack on one
  full attack, and extra attacks need not be taken in iterative order (paizo.com threads
  rzs2tmkp, rzs2lf75, rzs42eqh). Built so: `extra_attacks` is untyped (Rapid Shot) beside
  typed groups (ki, haste), and the order of swings is the plan's, not enforced.

## Shapes chosen, and why

- **A declared option is a `choice`**, the shape Power Attack already had (stage 8): a boolean
  on the attack op whose numbers are the feat document's, asked by `when.choice`. One reader
  (`Actor.can_choose`) for both feats and the monk's class feature.
- **"Until your next turn" is a one-round ActiveEffect carrying only a tag** (Combat
  Expertise, Cleave). The numbers stay in the document (`when.bearer.state`), so removing the
  effect removes them (law 2) and the formula is written once. Stage 8's plan anticipated
  exactly this for Combat Expertise ("a 1-round ActiveEffect ... a lifetime, so it is
  stored").
- **Extra swings are counts the sheet's plan reads**: `extra_attacks` (Rapid Shot, ki — the
  Battle Blood ki shape already shipped) and `off_hand_attacks` (the 0/-5/-10 ladder, shared
  by two-weapon fighting and the flurry). No second store: the plan is derived per attack.
- **A cleave's next blow is an engine-written attack in the queue**, the thrown flask's shape,
  so it suspends for the player's own die like any swing.

## Refused, with reasons

- Treating Power Attack-style options as lasting until the next turn on later rolls (Deadly
  Aim's "until your next turn" reaching a second ranged attack in the same round): each swing
  declares it; recorded in the document's `not_yet`.
- A persistent off-hand slot on the sheet: the off hand is declared with the full attack
  (`off_hand`), so no second equipped field and no save change. Its draw is not charged (see
  the lane report).
