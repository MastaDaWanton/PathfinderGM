# The surprise round: research before building (2026-11-22)

## The rule (primary)

Core Rulebook, Combat, "Surprise" (legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/combat.html, read
2026-11-22):

- "When a combat starts, if you are not aware of your opponents and they are aware of you,
  you're surprised." "Determining awareness may call for Perception checks or other checks."
- "If some but not all of the combatants are aware of their opponents, a surprise round
  happens before regular rounds begin." The aware act in initiative order, each taking "a
  standard or move action", plus free actions. "If no one or everyone starts the battle
  aware, there is no surprise round."
- "Combatants who are unaware at the start of battle don't get to act in the surprise
  round. Unaware combatants are flat-footed because they have not acted yet."
- Initiative, Flat-Footed: flat-footed until the first regular turn; uncanny dodge keeps Dex
  (it does not stop the surprise itself).

Not sourced: an official ruling on swift/immediate actions or a 5-foot step in the surprise
round. Not built either way: the engine counts a 5-foot step as the turn's move
(`Scene.move_spent`), as it already did.

## Prior art, and what was abandoned

| Tradition | Model | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Owlcat (Kingmaker, Wrath) | Party in stealth that sneaks up gets a surprise round; random encounters can open as an ambush on the party, and players report the party's Stealth/Perception setup changes how often | Player guides and Steam threads only (progameguides.com, steamcommunity.com); Owlcat documents none of it. Treated as unconfirmed. |
| Baldur's Gate 3 | `Surprised` condition: "cannot take any Actions or Reactions for one combat round"; decided by line of sight, and Stealth against Perception in obscured areas. Alert prevents it. | bg3.wiki "Surprise (game mechanic)". Patch 5 fixed it being applied twice; no removal found. |
| Foundry VTT PF1 | No surprise round in the core system that could be found; a third-party module ("PF1 Improved Conditions") ties it to flat-footed | foundryvtt-hub.com package page. Core system not confirmed either way. |
| D&D 5e 2024 | **Abandoned** the surprised condition: the unaware roll initiative with Disadvantage instead | 2024 PHB p.376 as quoted in coverage (comicbook.com, dungeonmister.com); the book itself not read |
| Pathfinder 2e | **Abandoned** the surprise round: Stealth (instead of Perception) is rolled for initiative | 2e.aonprd.com Rules ID 2423 |

Both abandonments change the rule rather than implement it. The owner asked for 1e by the
book, so the CRB's round is built. Recorded so the next person who asks "why not just roll
Stealth for initiative" finds the answer: that is 2e's rule, not ours.

## What was built (rules/surprise.py)

- One decision for every door a fight opens by: `Engine._ensure_encounter` (a swing, a cast,
  a splash, an ability, and every creature arrival) and `Engine._op_begin_encounter`.
- Awareness comes from `rules/awareness.py`: an observer is aware when it `sees` an opponent,
  or has a hunch (Ultimate Intrigue's "aware of presence"). An ambush door (`ambush=True`:
  the road, a journey, the hex, gathering in the wild, the water, the sea, a lair, a camp at
  night) adds the opposed roll: each ambusher's Stealth against each observer's Perception,
  -1 per 10 ft. All engine dice, hidden.
- The states are two conditions through the one applicator: `surprised`
  (`state.unable.surprised`) and `surprise round` (`turn.one-action`), each with one round
  on the one ticker. `Scene.end_encounter` lifts both.
- The one-action limit reads the turn's existing records (`move_spent`, `standard_spent`,
  the batch's resolved acts). The player's full attack or coup de grâce is refused with the
  reason. A creature's full attack becomes one blow.

Refused: a `Scene.surprise` flag or round counter, because it would be a second store beside
the conditions; letting the model's `begin_encounter.surprise` param decide awareness (no
model authors a state); re-rolling Perception against an already-settled Stealth hiding at
the fight's start (the hiding machinery already rolled it at each batch's end).
